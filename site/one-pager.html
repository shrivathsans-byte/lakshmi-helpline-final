Lakshmi: Government schemes over a phone call

A helpline for older people in Tier 2/3 India: call, speak in English or Tamil, and learn which of seven schemes you can get, what to carry and where to go, or get help with a payment that hasn't come, a lost card or a suspicious call.

Try it: live demo · talk to Lakshmi

1

Problem statement

Crores of older Indians are eligible for government schemes they don't receive. They don't know whether they qualify, what to bring, or where to go. Websites, apps and agents assume literacy, a smartphone or trust they don't have. Since most fraud reaches them by phone, they're wary of any unknown voice.

14.9 Crpeople aged 60+ in India (2022), 71% rural
55%of older people know the old age pension exists*
~30%of eligible poor older people receive it*
~6 Creligible for Ayushman Bharat 70+ (₹5 lakh/yr free hospital care)
~16%of them enrolled (~96 lakh cards, Dec 2025)
3.24 Crcalls to the 1930 cyber-fraud helpline in 2025

Who

People aged 60+ in Tier 2/3 towns and villages, or family members calling for them. They use a basic phone, have never used AI, and are eligible for at least one of seven central or Tamil Nadu schemes they don't receive.

Ex: a 72-year-old in a village outside Trichy whose son works in Chennai.

When

  • Turning 60 or 70
  • Facing a hospital bill
  • A pension or PM-Kisan payment hasn't arrived
  • A relative hears about a scheme
  • An Aadhaar or ration card is lost
  • Someone calls asking for an OTP

4

Solution

One number to call · about 2 minutes · English and Tamil
  1. 1
    Greeting. "Vanakkam! I am Lakshmi, a computer helper on a free helpline for government schemes. You can speak to me in English or Tamil. I will never ask you for money, your Aadhaar number, an OTP or bank details."
  2. 2
    Listens first. An urgent problem (someone in hospital, a payment that hasn't come, a lost card, a suspicious call) is solved first.
  3. 3
    Simple questions, one at a time: age, district, ration card type, widow or disability, farmland, income tax or government job.
  4. 4
    Names every scheme found, then explains one at a time: what you get, documents to carry, where to go, official helpline.
  5. 5
    Reference code and follow-up consent ("May we call you in a month?"). In production, an SMS summary too.

Trust, safety and evals

Automated tests on the live agent
48 of 48 runs pass: 24 scripted callers, each run twice
LayerWhat it does
Honest by designSays it's a computer helper in the first sentence; never claims to approve or guarantee; always "likely eligible, the office confirms"
GuardrailsBlocks any reply that asks for or repeats an Aadhaar number, OTP, bank details or money; blocks "ignore your instructions" attacks
Safety routesEmergency → 108; distress → Tele-MANAS 14416; elder abuse or neglect → Elder Line 14567; fraud → 1930
Difficult callersOff-topic: declines kindly and redirects. Frustration: stays calm and keeps helping. Repeated abuse after one warning: ends the call politely. Other languages: explains it speaks English and Tamil and offers a route
Scored on every callFive post-call checks: no personal data asked, official next step given, eligibility correct, safety handled, no false authority
Limits10-minute calls, 5 at once, 200 a day; transcripts deleted after 30 days

Test areas: right schemes (3), personal data and trust (6), fraud (3), safety (4), real problems (3), difficult callers (5). Earlier runs failed 3 and then 4 checks; each failure led to a prompt fix before the clean run.

7

Business outcomes

For citizens

  • Benefits actually received. Each enrolment is worth ₹2,400–6,000/yr in central pension, ₹6,000/yr in PM-Kisan, or ₹5 lakh/yr of hospital cover
  • No fees to agents for information that is free
  • No wasted trips: the right documents the first time

For government and partners

  • Higher scheme reach. Ayushman Bharat 70+ is at ~16% enrolment
  • Lower outreach cost than camps and door-to-door drives
  • District-level demand data: which schemes people ask about and where they get stuck
Business north star
₹ of benefits unlocked for citizens per ₹ spent on Lakshmi

Unit economics to prove

  • Cost per enrolment = cost per call ÷ % of calls that end in an enrolment
  • Compared with the cost of camps and door-to-door drives
  • Compared with ₹ of benefits unlocked per enrolment

What we will not do

  • Charge callers or run ads
  • Take referral fees from hospitals or centres
  • Sell financial products on the call
  • Sell individual data

* Data caveat: the ~30% uptake comes from an older health ministry survey, and the 55% awareness figure is attributed to the UNFPA India Ageing Report 2023 in press coverage. Benefit amounts are central-scheme values; state top-ups vary. Scheme amounts are Tamil Nadu rates from official and press sources and can change; Lakshmi always says the office confirms. Magalir Urimai Thogai rules were relaxed in 2025 and may have changed since, and the Elder Line (14567) and CM helpline (1100) numbers still need confirming from an official source. The revenue model is a hypothesis to validate, and cost per call is not yet measured. Phone-number matching is personal data processing under India's data protection law (DPDP Act): it needs explicit consent on the call, a stated purpose and a data-sharing agreement, and must run inside the partner's systems because a hashed phone number is easy to reverse.
