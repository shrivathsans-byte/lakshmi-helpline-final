# Lakshmi knowledge base (demo version, Tamil Nadu focus)

Compiled 22 Nov 2026 from official and press sources (PIB, NHA, pmkisan.gov.in, financialservices.gov.in, cra.tn.gov.in, uidai.gov.in, tnpds/NFSA, MoHFW). Amounts can change: always say "the office will confirm the exact amount".

## 1. Ayushman Bharat for people aged 70 and above (Ayushman Vay Vandana card)
- Who: every Indian citizen aged 70 or above. Age is the only condition, taken from the Aadhaar card. Income does not matter.
- What you get: free treatment in empanelled hospitals, up to 5 lakh rupees a year, as the senior's own cover. If the family already has an Ayushman card, the 70+ person gets their own extra cover of up to 5 lakh rupees a year.
- People with private health insurance can still get it. People in CGHS or ECHS must choose either that scheme or Ayushman Bharat, not both.
- What to carry: Aadhaar card, and the mobile phone linked to it (an OTP comes to that phone at the counter for e-KYC; type or show it only at the official counter or app, never share it on a call).
- Where to enrol: Ayushman app, beneficiary.nha.gov.in, a Common Service Centre or e-Sevai centre, or the health insurance desk of an empanelled hospital.
- Already in hospital: ask the hospital's health insurance desk (Ayushman Bharat / Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme) at once; enrolment can be done there. Helpline 14555.
- Helpline: 14555.

## 2. Old age pension (Tamil Nadu)
- Who: aged 60 or above, from a poor or destitute household (usually a Priority Household or Antyodaya ration card), low family income, no other government pension.
- What you get: about 1,200 rupees a month in Tamil Nadu (includes the central share). Paid into the bank account.
- Carry: Aadhaar card, age proof, ration card, bank passbook, passport-size photograph.
- Where: Taluk office (Tahsildar, social security section) or an e-Sevai centre.
- Helpline: e-Sevai helpdesk 1800 425 6000.

## 3. Destitute widow pension (Tamil Nadu)
- Who: widows from poor or destitute households with very little property. Women aged 60 or above usually get the old age pension instead. The Taluk office confirms the exact age rule.
- What you get: about 1,200 rupees a month. Paid into the bank account.
- Carry: husband's death certificate, Aadhaar card, ration card, bank passbook, passport-size photograph.
- Where: Taluk office or an e-Sevai centre. Helpline 1800 425 6000.

## 4. Differently abled pension (Tamil Nadu)
- Who: people with 40 percent or more disability, aged 18 or above, from poor households.
- What you get: about 1,500 rupees a month (Tamil Nadu rate since January 2023).
- Carry: disability certificate or UDID / national disability ID card, Aadhaar card, ration card, bank passbook, photograph.
- Where: Taluk office or an e-Sevai centre. Helpline 1800 425 6000.

## 5. Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai (Tamil Nadu women's monthly grant)
- Who: the woman head of the family on a ration card, aged 21 or above. Only one woman per ration card. Widows and unmarried women who head their household can apply. Other conditions apply (for example on income, land and electricity use) and were relaxed in 2025; the rules may have changed since, so the ration shop or e-Sevai centre confirms.
- What you get: 1,000 rupees a month into her bank account.
- Carry: ration card, Aadhaar card, bank passbook, photograph; a widow also carries her husband's death certificate.
- Where: the ration shop where the family card is registered, an e-Sevai centre, or kmut.tn.gov.in.

## 6. PM-Kisan (farm families)
- Who: families that own farmland in their own names (husband, wife, minor children).
- Not eligible if anyone in the family: paid income tax last year; is a practising doctor, engineer, lawyer, chartered accountant or architect; is a serving or retired government employee (some low-paid staff are exempt); holds a constitutional post or is or was an MP, MLA or minister.
- What you get: 6,000 rupees a year in three instalments of 2,000 rupees.
- Carry: Aadhaar card, bank passbook, land record (patta / chitta). e-KYC is required.
- Where: Common Service Centre / e-Sevai centre, or Farmers Corner on pmkisan.gov.in.
- Helpline: 155261.

## 7. PMSBY accident insurance (any adult with a bank account)
- Who: anyone aged 18 to 70 with a bank or post office savings account.
- What you get: 2 lakh rupees for accidental death or full disability, 1 lakh rupees for partial disability.
- Cost: 20 rupees a year, auto-debited from the bank account. Cover year is 1 June to 31 May.
- Where: fill the PMSBY form at your own bank branch or post office. Lakshmi never collects the 20 rupees and never takes bank details.

## 8. Common problems
- Payment has not come:
  - PM-Kisan: check e-KYC and that Aadhaar is linked to the bank account; call 155261.
  - Pension or women's grant: visit the Taluk office or e-Sevai centre with your bank passbook; helpdesk 1800 425 6000.
  - Never pay anyone to "release" a payment. Anyone asking for money or an OTP for this is a fraud.
- Lost Aadhaar card: call UIDAI on 1947, or visit an Aadhaar centre. A replacement PVC card can be ordered on the UIDAI website.
- Lost or damaged ration card: Tamil Nadu ration card helpline 1967 or 1800 425 5901, or the e-Sevai centre.
- Complaint about a scheme or office: Tamil Nadu Chief Minister's helpline 1100.

- Pensions, PM-Kisan, women's grant: the Taluk office of your taluk; e-Sevai centres (many are at Taluk offices); the ration shop for the women's grant.

## 10. Safety and emergencies
- Lakshmi never asks for money, Aadhaar number, OTP, PIN or bank details, and never collects payments.
- No government scheme asks for an OTP or money on a phone call. If someone does, it is a fraud: hang up and call 1930, or report at cybercrime.gov.in.
- Medical emergency, accident or someone in danger: call 108 now.
- Someone feeling hopeless, very low or thinking of harming themselves: Tele-MANAS, free and 24 hours, on 14416.
